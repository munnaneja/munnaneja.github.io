About

I study finance and information systems because I like sitting where the numbers meet the build. At Aquila Clouds, I spearhead top-of-funnel growth for AI FinOps and BillOps platforms - running targeted LinkedIn outreach, writing Python automation scripts, launching Meetup events, and building the trackers that turn activity into real pipeline.

Before that, I spent five weeks at the Mahama Refugee Camp in Rwanda drafting a legal agreement for Save the Children, helped two founders move from pre-seed to seed at the Founder Institute, and started a sliding-scale tutoring business while I was still in high school.

The thread running through all of it is simple: drop me somewhere new and I figure it out. Adaptability and resilience aren't lines on a page for me - they're how I've operated across very different rooms.

+ Adaptability

From a refugee camp in Kigali to a Bay Area FinOps startup, I've learned to read a new context fast, pick up the tools that matter, and start contributing before I feel ready.

+ Resilience

Building a business at 16, growing a fraternity chapter, coaching 50+ kids - I keep showing up through the unglamorous middle, because that's where most of the work actually gets done.
